North Deering · Portland, Maine

LysethRecords.org

In December 2006, Lyseth Elementary School Principal Phoebe Russell asked the State of Maine CDC to investigate the number of cancer cases in the school community. The state never finished or published a report on the rate of cancer in the Lyseth community. Why not?

"Elementary officials ask state to probe school, area cancer rates"

Josie Huang · Portland Press Herald · February 14, 2007

The article states Lyseth Elementary School Principal Phoebe Russell contacted the Maine Center for Disease Control requesting the CDC look into perceived elevated cancer rates at the school and surrounding neighborhood.

The numbers published state four students had developed cancer at the school in the past six years, one of which died. It goes on to state another 25 cases of cancer in the school community over the past decade were collected by the school. The Maine Cancer Registry will verify the 29 reported cases by the school against their database.

Lyseth Principal Phoebe Russell is quoted, "Our question is this: Is this an unusual number of cases for a community this size?"

Maine CDC Director Dr. Dora Anne Mills is paraphrased in the article stating the statistical likelihood of a cancer cluster is low. Director Mills is directly quoted, "People might say, 'I know 10 people with cancer' and then it turns out some of them have just moved into the community. They don't really count as a neighborhood cancer case."

Lyseth sent a note home to parents Friday (2/9/2007) to inform them of the "state inquiry and ask them to forward any information they might have". Director Mills is paraphrased stating the Maine Cancer Registry had all the information needed, and the school's collection of data was unnecessary. "There is no timeline for when the state will report its findings."
