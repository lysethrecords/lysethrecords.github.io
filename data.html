North Deering Neighborhood Cancer Data

Data provided by Maine CDC (report Tables 1–29), organized by age group. Rates are compared to North Deering.

How to read the data: For each cancer site North Deering's rate and case count are shown at the top of the table. Rate per 100k is a standardized measurement to compare different size populations. "Expected Count" is how many cases North Deering would have if cancer occured at the same rate as the comparison population. The p-value is the likelihood of the result occurring by random chance — the smaller the p-value the less likely the result; below 0.05 is considered statistically significant, echoed in the Result column. Population is the actual population multiplied by 10 (years studied); in other words, each person is counted each year.
A note on the probability values: The original data file had an error in its probability column: =POISSON(observed, expected, FALSE). The FALSE returns the probability of exactly the observed count, not the cumulative tail a significance test requires. Changing the "FALSE" to "TRUE" in the formula fixes the error. The p-values here are recalculated as a two-sided Poisson test. Original data available in the Reports & Data section.
